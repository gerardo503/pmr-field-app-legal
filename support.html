Support & Contact

Need help with PMR Field App? Email dev@pmrroofing.com and include your name, your crew and what you were doing. Screenshots help.

Common questions

I can't sign in. Accounts are created by PMR. Ask your manager to check that your account exists and is assigned to a crew.

I see no jobs. You may not be assigned to a crew or to a route today. Contact the office.

My photos/actions didn't send. The app queues them without signal and sends them when the connection returns. Keep the app installed and open it once you have signal.

Notifications don't arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your device settings.
