Request Account and Data Deletion

PMR Field App accounts are created and managed by PMR, so deletion is handled by request.

How to request

  1. Email dev@pmrroofing.com with the subject "Delete my PMR Field App account" from your work email, or ask your PMR manager.
  2. We verify your identity and confirm within 30 days.

What happens when you request deletion

Your account is deactivated immediately: you can no longer sign in, and you stop appearing as an active crew member. Your registered device stops receiving push notifications right away.

Location data shared while live tracking is active is kept for a maximum of 3 days before it is automatically and permanently removed, regardless of any deletion request.

Today, we retain your name, email and job/work records (check-ins, notes, photos, messages) after deactivation, for recordkeeping, warranty and legal purposes. We are working on a process to fully erase or anonymize that personal data on request; until it ships, contact us at the email below and we will handle it manually as far as our systems and legal obligations allow.

See also the Privacy Policy.
