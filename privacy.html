Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what data PMR Field App (package com.pmrroofing.fieldapp), operated by PMR Roofing ("PMR", "we"), collects, why, and your choices. The app is a work tool for PMR employees and authorized partners; accounts are created and managed by PMR.

Data we collect

DataWhyWhen
Name, work email, account and user IDSign in; show who checked in, who sent a message, who took a photoWhen your account is used
Precise locationShow your position on the route map and, if enabled by PMR, share it with the office during an active job visitOnly while the app is open (foreground) and only after you grant permission. The app never collects location in the background
PhotosDocument jobs (before/after, incidents), attached to a specific jobOnly photos you choose to take or select
Messages, notes, incident reports, expensesCommunicate with the office and record work performedWhen you submit them
Push notification tokenDeliver notifications about jobs and messagesIf you allow notifications

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and the app does not include crash-reporting services from third parties.

How we use it

Only to operate the app's functions described above and to run PMR's business (scheduling, job documentation, safety and customer service). We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps.

Who receives it

Data is sent over HTTPS to PMR's own servers (PMR Connect, hosted on Amazon Web Services), and is visible to authorized PMR staff. Push notifications are delivered through Apple's and Google's notification services, which receive the notification token and message content needed for delivery. Map tiles are provided by Google Maps. We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention

Precise location shared while live tracking is active is kept for a maximum of 3 days, then automatically and permanently deleted. Your push notification token is removed as soon as you sign out or your device unregisters.

Account data and job records (name, email, check-ins, notes, photos, messages) are kept while your account is active and after deactivation, for as long as PMR needs them for business, legal and warranty purposes. Today, deactivating an account stops sign-in and notifications immediately, but does not automatically erase your name and email from our records — see Request account and data deletion for how to ask us to do that.

Your choices and rights

  • Deny or revoke camera, photo, location or notification permission in your device settings; the rest of the app continues to work.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your data: see Request account and data deletion or contact us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Sign-in tokens are stored in the device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to authorized staff.

Children

The app is for adults only and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change.
